Breakeven definition

The required trip growth matches the existing policy’s contribution, rather than zero profit. The extra incentive applies to all forecast trips. At the exact threshold, contribution matches baseline. Higher growth improves contribution under these assumptions.

Final project conclusion: $0.60 is a candidate for a future experiment if it can produce more than approximately 4.15% extra trips. This project does not establish that response. Threshold comparisons use full precision. Power planning and real rollout approval remain future work.
